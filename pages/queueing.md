# Queueing Theory

Queueing theory studies waiting lines: arrivals, service, and the resulting congestion. A few objects I keep coming back to:

## Glossary

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| $\lambda$ | arrival rate |
| $\mu$ | service rate |
| $\rho = \lambda / \mu$ | traffic intensity |
| $L$ | mean number in system |
| $W$ | mean time in system |

## Little's law

For a stable system,

$$ L = \lambda W $$

The same relationship holds for the queue (excluding service) as $L_q = \lambda W_q$.
