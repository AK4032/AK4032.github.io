## Differential Varshamov–Tenengolts codes

Differential Varshamov–Tenengolts (D-VT) codes are $q$-ary codes that correct a single deletion (or insertion). In [On Differential Varshamov–Tenengolts Codes](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISIT63088.2025.11195208) we:

- determine the sizes of D-VT codes;
- clarify their relationship to Tenengolts codes, which gives a more efficient decoder;
- characterize certain D-VT codes in terms of $q$-ary necklaces.
