I wanted to share that I've created my own website! This is it. The color palette is a nice green and purple with a gradient as the headboard! I tried using Google Sites to create a website too. I'm not sure which one will turn out better or become my actual website.

The reason why I wanted to make one is because I'm a junior in college and have been looking at PhD programs to apply to! Many professors I admire, including both my advisors, have super cool websites with a lot of tabs and info about several different things. I've also been looking at students, and while it seems to be standard for grad students (especially PhD students) to have their own websites, many of my undergrad friends here in SD have there own cool-looking websites, too. A lot of them go into detail about various projects that they've did, whether on their own or as a group with someone else. My first ever research conference is upcoming, so if I meet any cool people there to explain my research to, I can refer them to this website as well!

Webdev is hard. I struggled thinking about how to design this. I knew I always liked top-to-bottom color gradients, but I'm not sure why I chose bright green and purple for this one? It could possibly be because neither of those colors are ones I traditionally associated with 'myself', and since making this website and possibly going to grad school after I graduate will be a new adventure, these nontraditional colors could symbolize a new path I could be taking.
